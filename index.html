About the Company

Rooted in Wyoming, reaching toward the horizon

Green Horizon Farms LLC was founded in 2024 and registered with the State of Wyoming — a state known for its open land, favorable business environment, and deep agricultural heritage.

Our mission is to develop sustainable, forward-thinking agricultural operations that respect the land while building lasting economic value. We are positioned to serve domestic and international markets with integrity and expertise.

Headquartered in Sheridan, Wyoming, we operate at the intersection of traditional farming values and modern agribusiness innovation.
